# HireMind AI Recruiter User Guide
## Hiring Workflows, Resume Processing, & AI Rankings

This guide assists recruiters in leveraging AI models to optimize their hiring workflow pipeline.

---

## 1. Creating Job Openings
1. Navigate to the **Job Openings** dashboard.
2. Click **Create Job** and fill out the details (Job Title, Requirements description, Department).
3. The platform parses requirements automatically to generate a baseline embedding vector target.

---

## 2. Parsing Resumes & Candidate Onboarding
1. Drag and drop resumes in PDF format in the **Resume Upload** section.
2. The background parser extracts work histories, educations, and coding skills.
3. Candidates are matched against open job embeddings using vector similarity scoring.

---

## 3. Reviewing AI Evaluations
- **ATS Match Score**: Evaluates skill requirements, generating a matching score from 0% to 100%.
- **Drift Auditing**: Tracks candidate score variations to ensure model grading remains consistent.
- **Stage Progression**: Drag and drop candidates across the visual recruitment stages pipeline (Screening, Interview, Offer, Hired).
